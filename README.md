# Dodge the Animals — 3D prototype

A test build of Group A's student-designed game. The rules and difficulty settings are provisional until students playtest and revise them.

## Play

Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. Hold Space to run on the spot, then use Left/Right or A/D to dodge and collect stars. On a touch screen, hold Run and tap the arrow buttons. Camera mode is experimental. Keep 2 m of clear space. Use STOP or Esc to pause.

The game uses Three.js from jsDelivr. Camera mode downloads MediaPipe assets from jsDelivr; video frames are processed in the browser. Voice recognition may use a browser online service. The game does not store names or scores. The how-to video is a placeholder until `howto.mp4` is supplied.
